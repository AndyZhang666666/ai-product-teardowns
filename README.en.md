# AI Product Teardowns

[中文](README.md) · [Project index](https://github.com/AndyZhang666666/AndyZhang666666/blob/main/PROJECTS.en.md)

If I owned this product, what would I change and how would I judge it?

## Product decisions

- Compare scenarios, capability boundaries, failure handling and monetisation with one framework.
- Separate external observations from implementation hypotheses; no implied insider knowledge.
- Include a disagreement and a concrete next step in each analysis.

## Walkthrough

1. Read FRAMEWORK.md for the six fixed dimensions.
2. Compare the dated Kling, Doubao and Wenku AI analyses, including value and payment moments.
3. Use TEMPLATE.md for a new teardown with explicit observation dates and evidence.

## Run mode and limits

A writing project, not a runnable demo. The three analyses describe their observation period; current products may differ. Architecture hypotheses and proposed metrics are not official facts.

## Read the analyses

- [Kling](teardowns/kling.md): controllability and the cost of failed generations.
- [Doubao](teardowns/doubao.md): setting expectations and finding a value moment.
- [Wenku AI](teardowns/wenku-ai.md): generation inside an existing paid funnel.

The articles are in Chinese. They use public information and ordinary product use; the author’s Baidu internship is not evidence of access to internal product data.
